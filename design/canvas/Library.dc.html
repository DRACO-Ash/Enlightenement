Manoeuvre detection and characterisation

manoeuvre-detection v1 · active · illustrative draft, pending authoring

Establish whether an observed change in an object's orbit is a deliberate manoeuvre, characterise it, and decide whether it meets the threshold for reporting.

Steps

  1. Confirm the change is present in more than one independent fit before treating it as real. Analyst on watch Note: A single fit can move on data quality alone. Two fits from different epochs is the cheapest discriminator available.
  2. Rule out a data artefact: check epoch spacing, near-duplicate epochs, and whether the residuals are consistent with the contributing sensors. Analyst on watch Warning: A very small epoch gap between fits produces an implied drift rate that is physically impossible. Treat an implausible rate as an artefact hypothesis of equal standing, not a footnote.
  3. Estimate the along-track and radial change and derive an approximate delta-v. Analyst on watch Note: Judge the figure against what the platform class could plausibly produce.
  4. Classify the manoeuvre as station-keeping, repositioning, or unattributed. Analyst on watch Note: Station-keeping is periodic and small. Repositioning changes the drift rate and holds the change.
  5. Check the object against the protected-object criteria held outside this application before deciding the reporting threshold. Watch lead Warning: This application never holds that list. Knowing the check exists and is mandatory is the trained behaviour; performing it requires the authoritative source.

Steps 6 and 7, thresholds, reporting requirements and closure criteria follow.

Seven markers, and what each one asks of you

Every marker carries a glyph, a word and a colour. Nothing in this library means anything by colour alone, so a monochrome print and a screen read identically.

One thing this design refuses to do

The content orders decision points but does not bind them to a step number. A flowchart that placed a diamond between step 6 and step 7 would be inventing a sequence the authors did not write, so decisions keep their own track and the map says so. When the content grows a step reference, the diamonds move onto the spine and nothing else changes.
